Our Heritage

180 Years of Tea Excellence

Sylhet & Sreemangal Estates

A Legacy Rooted in the Finest Terroir

Nestled in the misty highlands of Sylhet and the verdant slopes of Chittagong, RUHA represents the pinnacle of Bangladeshi tea craftsmanship — a tradition spanning over 180 years.

We partner exclusively with heritage estates where generations of master artisans hand-select only the most pristine leaves at dawn, when essential oils and polyphenol concentrations are at their absolute peak.

Every batch undergoes our exacting 12-point quality protocol — from garden to export — ensuring that the tea arriving at your facility is nothing less than extraordinary.
