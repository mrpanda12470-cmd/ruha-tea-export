Our Heritage

Rooted in the lush, rolling hills of Sylhet and Chittagong, RUHA carries forward the rich legacy of Bangladesh's tea heritage. For generations, our fertile soils and unique monsoon climate have produced some of the world's finest teas.

We work directly with historic tea estates, ensuring that every leaf is plucked at the peak of perfection. Our commitment to quality, sustainability, and ethical farming practices allows us to deliver an authentic taste of Bangladesh to connoisseurs across the globe.

From the garden to your cup, we oversee every step of the process with meticulous care, guaranteeing unmatched freshness and flavor in every batch we export.
