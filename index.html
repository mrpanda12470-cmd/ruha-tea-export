A Grand Terroir Defined by Mist, Monsoon & Volcanic Soil

Since the establishment of the subcontinent's grandest estates in the 1850s, the undulating contours of Sylhet and Chittagong have produced teas revered for their unmatched liquor body, copper reflections, and malty honey notes.

At RUHA, we steward this heritage with unrelenting discipline. We operate exclusively with historic gardens situated on high-altitude microclimates, where tropical monsoon cloud cover filters sunlight, yielding leaves enriched with deep catechins and natural sweetness.

Monsoon Rhythms

Over 4,000mm of gentle annual rainfall creates rich natural sap and tender foliage.

Acidic Highland Loam

Pristine, mineral-laden humus soils providing signature briskness and depth.

Dawn Harvest Windows

Plucked strictly between 5:30 AM and 9:00 AM before heat dissipates delicate aromatics.

100% Traceability
